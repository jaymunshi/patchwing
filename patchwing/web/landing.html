Finding vulnerabilities isn't the bottleneck.

Verified fixing is.

PatchWing takes a known vulnerability and produces a fix a maintainer can merge: a reproducer that fails before and passes after, a minimal patch, a regression test, the project's own suite still green, and a human sign-off. Not another scanner — a closure pipeline.

Sandboxed proof

Every reproducer and verification runs in a disposable container with no network. The bug is demonstrated on real code, not asserted.

Bring your own model

Per-role endpoints for any OpenAI-compatible provider — Together, OpenAI, Grok, local Ollama — plus native Claude. Keys stay on your box. Set it on the Providers page.

Adversarial by design

Fixer and verifier are different model families, so the second opinion doesn't share the first's blind spots. A machine never signs off its own work.

Definition-A only

Reproducers demonstrate a trigger and an observable boundary violation, and stop there — exploitability proof, nothing weaponized beyond it.
